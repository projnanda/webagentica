## 1. CPython

`openclaw-agent-browser/src/html-entities.ts` is generated by
`openclaw-agent-browser/scripts/generate-html-entities.py` from three tables of CPython 3.13's `html`
package: `html.entities.html5` (`Lib/html/entities.py`), and `html._invalid_charrefs` and
`html._invalid_codepoints` (`Lib/html/__init__.py`). Regenerating it with CPython 3.13.5
reproduces the committed file byte for byte. CPython's `html.entities.html5` table is itself
generated from the WHATWG HTML Standard's list of named character references
(`https://html.spec.whatwg.org/entities.json`), as stated in CPython's source.

`openclaw-agent-browser/src/html-text.ts` ports to TypeScript the text extraction that CPython
3.13 performs with `html.parser.HTMLParser(convert_charrefs=True)` followed by `html.unescape`. Its
regular expressions are transcribed from `Lib/html/parser.py`, `Lib/_markupbase.py` and
`Lib/html/__init__.py`.

Summary of changes made to the CPython material:

- The three tables are re-expressed as TypeScript `Map` and `Set` literals; their entries are
  unchanged.
- The regular expressions are rewritten in JavaScript `RegExp` syntax, with `\s` expanded to the
  characters for which Python's `str.isspace()` is true.
- Only the parsing logic needed to extract text is ported, for the single configuration
  `convert_charrefs=True` with no final `close()` call; other parser callbacks are not ported.
- Where CPython raises `AssertionError` on markup it cannot parse (for example a marked section
  with an unknown keyword, `<![foo[`), the port returns `null` for that field instead.

## 4. Scholarly metadata in recorded runs and test fixtures

The recorded runs and verification evidence under `examples/` and the Crossref fixtures under
`openclaw-agent-browser/test/fixtures/` contain bibliographic metadata (titles, author names,
identifiers, venues), abstracts, and short quotations from those abstracts, as returned by Crossref,
arXiv and OpenAlex while the software was being tested. They are kept as evidence of what the
software received and produced. They are not part of any release artifact, they are not covered by
this repository's license, and any rights in them remain with their respective owners.

Package fixtures (`agents/*/fixtures/`), the probe PDF in `examples/probe-files/`, and the test PDFs
generated by the test suites are synthetic.
