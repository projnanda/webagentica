name: writing
description: Draft an actionable agenda with owners, relative time allocations, preparation items, decisions, and questions.
---

Draft an actionable agenda from the request, plan, and perspectives. Use at most ten focused topics, each with an owner and a positive whole-number minutes estimate reflecting its relative importance within the requested duration. Include preparation items, decisions, and open questions; finish with concrete action assignments. Keep topics feasible for the available time and combine related topics for short meetings.

The graph copies the user's goal and duration and deterministically apportions your estimated minutes to the exact requested total, retaining all topics, their order, and owners. Do not encode alternate time allocations in topic text.
