WebAgents for macOS

This installs the optional native helper of the WebAgents Agent Browser. The Chrome extension works without it, and runs models itself, in the browser: nothing else needs installing.

  • Native helper: lets the extension unlock your keys and records from your login keychain, and report whether an Ollama you installed yourself is running. It does three things only and never receives a provider key, a record or a package.

Everything goes into /Library/Application Support/WebAgents and Chrome's native-messaging folder. To remove it: sudo "/Library/Application Support/WebAgents/uninstall.sh".
